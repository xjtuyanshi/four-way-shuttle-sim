  Source: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Fabric001
- `ambientcg-rubber001/*`: Rubber001 from ambientCG, CC0 1.0 Universal.
  Source: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rubber001
- `polyhaven-metal-plate/*`: Metal Plate from Poly Haven, CC0.
  Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate
